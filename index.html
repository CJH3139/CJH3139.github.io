Tappy

An idle clicker you play in vanilla Minecraft

Tap emeralds, hire mobs, get rich. The camera is locked onto a flat screen built entirely from display entities, and your mouse drives an on-screen cursor. No mods and no resource pack needed.

  • Custom server on Minestom, with every player's world ticking in parallel
  • Optional Smooth Cursor pack draws the cursor on your GPU with core shaders, cutting 115 to 165 ms of lag
  • Plays on 1.19.4 and up through a Velocity and ViaVersion proxy
